01 — About

I'm a self-taught builder who fell for the craft of turning ideas into working systems.

For nearly five years, I built and operated the systems that decide what a customer sees, when, and why — campaigns across enterprise marketing automation platforms, running at the scale of Fortune 500 accounts. I got good at reading data and automating the parts of a workflow that didn't need a human in the loop. What I didn't have was the ability to build the systems myself.

CS50x taught me to think from first principles — pointers and memory one week, algorithms and SQL the next. I like problems where good engineering and clear communication meet, which is exactly where my favorite projects live.
