Q2
The biggest issue is that averaging just kind of erases all the variety. If this person listened to 50 super happy songs and 50 sad ones,
the average would land right in the middle and make it look like they only listen to "okay" music — which isn't true at all.
It also doesn't care how many times each song was actually played. A song that snuck into the top 100 with one or two plays gets weighted exactly the same as a song they had on repeat for weeks.
I think a fairer approach would be to weight each song by play count, so the songs someone is actually obsessed with pull the average more than the ones they barely listened to.
It'd also be worth looking at the spread of the data, not just the average — someone who's all over the place emotionally is very different from someone who's consistently in one mood, even if their averages look identical.
